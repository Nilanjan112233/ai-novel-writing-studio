# Phase 7 Canon Proposal Walkthrough

AI-generated facts and author-created suggestions enter **Canon Queue** as pending proposals. Each proposal shows type, subject and field, proposed value, confidence, evidence, affected chapters/scenes, and the existing Change Impact preview.

- **Approve** promotes the fact through Canon Governance, rebuilds the Story Bible, and incrementally refreshes Narrative Analysis.
- **Reject** records the decision and leaves canon unchanged.
- High-impact proposals cannot be approved until the author explicitly confirms the displayed downstream impact.

The queue accepts only supported canon types. Approved facts retain proposal provenance, so later conflicts and explanations can identify their source.
