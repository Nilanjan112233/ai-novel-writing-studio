# NovelForge Workspace Companion Role Report

## Role

Companion remains a lightweight status and next-action helper. Deep story conversation remains in Story Studio.

## Live behavior

After Chapter 2 approval, Companion surfaced `Continue with Chapter 3` and linked back to the Writing workspace. Dashboard and Writing agreed that Chapter 2 was the latest approved chapter.

During the live run it also reported two new companion messages while approved-chapter suggestions were available. This was useful as a signal, but the count is still more operational than author-friendly.

## Recommendation

Keep Companion compact and contextual. It should summarize pending review, background work, warnings, and the next useful action; it should not grow into a second conversational assistant.
