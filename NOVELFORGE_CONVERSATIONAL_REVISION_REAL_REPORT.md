# NovelForge Conversational Revision Report

## Result

Story Studio can prepare a protected revision from a chapter-specific natural-language request.

## Real workflow

The browser request “Chapter 1 is too dramatic in the second half. Keep the funeral and the Elias mystery, but make the conflict quieter and more unsettling.” created an Editorial branch and RevisionAgent version through real OpenRouter/DeepSeek calls. Story Studio reported that the approved source was unchanged and directed the author to review the alternative.

## Preserved constraints

The request, chapter source snapshot, protected planning/canon/timeline elements, and chapter outcome are carried into the existing Editorial context. The author can compare, reject, or deliberately use the revised version. No direct chat overwrite was introduced.
