# Novel Creation Guide

## Purpose

The guide turns NovelForge into a navigable production pipeline for new authors while allowing experienced authors to jump directly to any step. Completion is evidence-derived; it is not a manual checklist.

## Guided sequence

1. Project creation
2. Story vision
3. Genre
4. Theme
5. Characters
6. Worldbuilding
7. Power system
8. Relationships
9. Timeline
10. Arcs
11. Scene planning
12. Paragraph drafting
13. Revision
14. Story Health
15. Canon review
16. Publication readiness

Every step explains why it matters, which AI systems participate, required information, optional improvements, current completion, blockers, and the direct navigation target.

## Production gates

### Concept Complete

The project has a usable story vision, genre/themes, and central character intent.

### World Ready

Core world rules, relevant power-system decisions, primary relationships, and foundational canon are defined.

### Draft Ready

Major arcs, chronology, active mysteries, and scene-level planning are sufficiently established for sustained drafting.

### Revision Ready

A draft exists, Story Intelligence has completed, and critical structural evidence is available for revision.

### Publication Ready

There are no critical canon or timeline conflicts, major arcs and mysteries meet their resolution criteria, high-severity Story Health findings are below the configured threshold, and canon review is complete.

Each gate exposes its explicit criteria and blockers. The workspace shows the current gate and the highest-value next action, not only a percentage.

## Freedom and author control

Authors may jump between steps. Optional genres and story forms can mark irrelevant criteria as not applicable. The guide never generates prose or applies narrative changes without the existing author approval workflows.
