# NovelForge Creative Distinctiveness and Repetition Report

## Implemented

Story Studio review requests now route to the existing Narrative Critique / Narrative Analysis ownership. The checks include:

- repeated prose patterns;
- repeated chapter openings;
- generic prose fingerprints;
- weak chapter hooks and endings;
- repetitive scenes and exposition patterns;
- evidence-backed current story findings.

## Live evidence

The browser review of Chapter 1 returned a concrete warning: multiple sentences begin with a similar grammatical shape. A separate opening-focused review identified the funeral hook, Mara's fractured perspective, the debt metaphor, and the forward question around the safe-deposit key.

The system did not compare the work against every novel ever written. Distinctiveness is reported from confirmed project elements and current story evidence.

## Guidance style

Recommendations remain optional and are grounded in the project's own chapters, characters, world facts, mysteries, and analysis. They do not mutate prose or planning automatically.

## Limitation

The current checks are deterministic/analysis-backed signals, not a complete literary originality evaluator. Genre familiarity, voice, and commercial appeal still require author judgment.
