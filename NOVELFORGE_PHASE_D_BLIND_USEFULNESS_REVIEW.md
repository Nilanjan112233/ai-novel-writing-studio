# Phase D Blind Usefulness Review

## Procedure

The three specialist-output bundles were anonymized as Packet X/Y/Z before review. The manual rubric was completed without arm labels, then the separately stored map was revealed. The generating Gemini model was not used as the sole judge.

Revealed mapping:

- Packet X: current Phase C
- Packet Y: fact-only
- Packet Z: deterministic progressive

Scores use a 1–5 scale.

| Dimension | Current | Fact only | Progressive |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Usefulness to author | 4.5 | 3.0 | 4.0 |
| Specificity | 5.0 | 2.5 | 4.0 |
| Narrative coherence | 4.0 | 3.5 | 4.0 |
| Cross-domain integration | 4.5 | 3.0 | 4.0 |

| Author-intent preservation | 3.5 | 3.5 | 3.0 |
| **Mean** | **4.111** | **3.000** | **3.889** |

## Blind observations

Current was the most concrete and actionable. It made effective use of the ledger, safe-deposit box, Lindon debt, mill, and Mara/Ivo interaction. Its weakness was over-committing newly proposed mystery truths instead of labelling them consistently as proposals.

Fact-only was coherent but generic. Missing episodes caused it to invent broad connective material rather than develop established interactions and clues. This confirms the readiness audit's fact-only failure.

Progressive was markedly better than fact-only and had the strongest contradiction awareness. It preserved the five-year authority and Chapter 10 boundary. It still lost enough clue detail to be less specific and less useful than current, repeated the chronology correction across roles, and occasionally over-stated the possibility that Elias is alive.

## Gate

- Blind usefulness non-inferior: **FAIL** (4.0 versus 4.5).
- Blind overall mean non-inferior: **FAIL** (3.889 versus 4.111).
- Coherence non-inferior: pass.
- Contradiction awareness: improved.

The pilot must not be accepted on token savings alone.
