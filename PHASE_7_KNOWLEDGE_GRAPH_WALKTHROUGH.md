# Phase 7 Knowledge Graph Walkthrough

Open **Knowledge Graph** from the project toolbox or choose **Graph** in the AI Narrative Companion.

The graph displays characters, relationships, locations, organizations, powers, artifacts, mysteries, plot threads, timeline events, and canon facts directly from Narrative Analysis nodes and edges. Use the kind filter or search box to narrow large projects.

Selecting a node opens its summary, source, connected entities, chapter and scene locations, timeline/canon references, and associated Story Health warnings. Relationship and warning changes appear after the next Narrative Analysis refresh; Phase 7 does not maintain a duplicate graph.
