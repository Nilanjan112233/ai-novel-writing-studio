# Phase D Specialist Quality Comparison

## Method

Each specialist was checked against five role-specific labelled requirements. These checks assess required facts and planning behaviors; they do not substitute for the separate blind author-usefulness review.

| Specialist | Current | Fact only | Progressive | Finding |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Relationship | 5/5 | 4/5 | 5/5 | Episodes restored prior-interaction continuity. |
| Character Journey | 5/5 | 4/5 | 5/5 | Episodes restored state-change continuity and consequences. |
| Mystery | 3/5 | 4/5 | 4/5 | Progressive fixed five-year chronology, but neither compact arm fully controlled premature proposal-as-answer behavior. |
| Arc Architect | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 | All arms met the coarse requirements; specificity differed in blind review. |
| Pacing | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 | All met rubric terms; current/progressive were more grounded than fact-only. |
| **Total** | **23/25** | **22/25** | **24/25** | Progressive passed deterministic non-inferiority. |

## Role findings

### Relationship

Fact-only knew the requested direction but lacked enough interaction history to make the progression feel earned. The progressive episode restored the existing friction and movement. Current and progressive both passed 5/5.

### Character Journey

Fact-only preserved motivation and consequences in general terms but lost episode continuity. Progressive recovered that context. Its output was more grounded than fact-only, though still less concrete than current in the blind packet-level review.

### Mystery

This role lost the most useful information. The progressive projection preserved the central mystery, five-year authority, Chapter 10 boundary, Lindon red-herring role, and some locations. It omitted several concrete clue/evidence details available to current Phase C. The result remained actionable but less specific.

The progressive output correctly named the two-year derived episode as a contradiction. It nevertheless converted some interpretations into planning certainty, a behavior also present in the current arm. This is an output-governance issue, not evidence that the contradictory value overrode authority.

### Arc Architect

Progressive retained causal progression and character/mystery integration. It used earlier handoffs correctly. Its plan was viable, but repeated chronology correction consumed attention that could have been spent on richer causal detail.

### Pacing

Progressive retained escalation and reveal timing. Its directive to “purge” two-year references was too strong: derived historical evidence should be marked conflicting/superseded, not destructively removed. No deletion occurred; this was only generated advice.

## Interpretation

The deterministic rubric alone would accept progressive. The blind review prevents that false positive. Keyword/requirement coverage can remain complete while the evidence packet loses the exact clues that make specialist advice useful and non-generic.

Recommended next experiment: retain deterministic role routing, but expand the Mystery projection with high-value clue, obligation, location, and evidence-reference fields; present the chronology conflict once in shared context or only to roles that must resolve it.
