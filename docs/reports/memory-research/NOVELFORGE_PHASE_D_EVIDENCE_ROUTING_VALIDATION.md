# Phase D Evidence Routing Validation

## Deterministic matched route

Every active specialist was classified as episode-required for this instruction. No matched-arm role was classified as wording-dependent, so no raw prose was preloaded.

| Role | Selected depth | Facts | Episodes | Why |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Relationship | Fact + episode | 250 | 625 | Trust progression depends on prior interactions and state movement. |
| Character Journey | Fact + episode | 250 | 662 | Motivation and consequence require the sequence of character changes. |
| Mystery | Fact + episode | 250 | 574 | Clue evolution, red herrings, and reveal boundaries are temporal. |
| Arc Architect | Fact + episode | 261 | 761 | Causal integration needs event/arc progression across domains. |
| Pacing | Fact + episode | 261 | 599 | Escalation and repetition require chapter/scene sequence, not isolated facts. |

The routing classification was correct at the level of evidence depth: fact-only lost continuity and became generic, while episodes restored all labelled depth requirements for Relationship and Character Journey and improved the overall deterministic score.

The selected episode contents were not yet sufficient for production. In particular, the Mystery projection was too compressed to retain the concrete clue set that made current Phase C more useful.

## Conflict handling

The shared authority states that Elias disappeared five years ago. A derived episode contains a two-year observation. Progressive:

- retained five years as authoritative;
- labelled two years as conflicting derived evidence;
- used the conflict where relevant;
- never promoted two years to equal authority;
- achieved five of five output-level chronology checks.

The conflict note was over-distributed. Future routing should include it once in shared authority/conflict context, with targeted expansion for Mystery/Arc roles.

## Raw-evidence branch

A separate wording-specific real-provider test asked whether Chapter 1 river foreshadowing was subtle.

| Variant | Prompt | Response | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Episode only | 927 | 184 | Correctly reported insufficient wording evidence. |
| Episode + bounded prose | 1,092 | 250 | Correctly assessed subtle personification using exact supplied phrases. |

The raw passage materially changed answerability. This validates raw expansion for wording-level tasks and argues against preloading raw prose for ordinary planning roles.

## Determinism

Each role's manifest was generated twice. All five pairs had identical hashes and unique selected evidence IDs. Deterministic routing is mechanically sufficient; no model-requested retrieval tool is justified by this pilot.

## Recommendation

Keep deterministic role-aware routing as the experimental direction, but revise the evidence fields and rerun the pilot. Do not deploy the current manifests.
