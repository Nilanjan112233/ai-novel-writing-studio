## Comparative result

- Blind narrative coherence: current 4.0, fact-only 3.5, progressive 4.0.
- Blind cross-domain integration: current 4.5, fact-only 3.0, progressive 4.0.
- No missing, duplicated, or out-of-order handoff was observed.
- Progressive did not disconnect the overall plan.

Progressive's lower packet-level usefulness did not come from broken handoffs. It came from the evidence selected before and around those handoffs: later agents received coherent deltas, but Mystery and downstream roles had fewer concrete clues to integrate.

## Repetition and drift

The chronology conflict appeared prominently in multiple progressive role outputs. That repetition did not break coherence, but it displaced role-specific reasoning and led Pacing to recommend destructive-sounding “purge” language. A future pilot should carry one compact conflict label with evidence references, then expose additional conflict detail only to roles responsible for chronology/mystery resolution.

## Conclusion

Cross-agent coherence is non-inferior, but it does not rescue the failed overall quality gate. The existing delta-handoff system is compatible with deterministic progressive evidence; the role evidence projection needs refinement.
