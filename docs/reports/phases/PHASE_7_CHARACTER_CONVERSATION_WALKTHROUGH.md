# Phase 7 Character Conversation Walkthrough

1. Open a project and select **AI Narrative Companion**.
2. Choose **Characters**, select a Story Bible character, and choose the conversation chapter.
3. Ask a question or interview the character. NovelForge loads the character's personality, motivations, beliefs, relationships, emotional state, dialogue style, vocabulary, conflicts, prior interaction memory, approved canon, and events known at that point.
4. The response displays its awareness chapter and a death-boundary badge when chronology capped the context.
5. Promises, opinions, relationship changes, emotions, and unresolved conflicts returned by the interaction are retained as character conversation memory and included in the Story Bible's interactive-intelligence section.
6. Any new narrative fact is placed in the Canon Queue. It is not official canon until the author approves it.

The browser certification conversed with Mara Venn from a requested Chapter 20 position after her Chapter 3 death. The response was generated from Chapter 3 awareness, excluded a Chapter 12 fact, and displayed **Death boundary enforced**.
