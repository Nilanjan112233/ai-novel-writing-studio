## Acceptance result

The workflow is operational and safe for a real two-chapter trial. It is not yet a proof of perfect long-form continuity: Chapter 2's prose says Elias disappeared two years ago while confirmed planning says five years, and one derived candidate incorrectly describes Mara as Lindon's brother. Both remained candidate/derived rather than canon, but they demonstrate a continuity/extraction quality gap that must remain visible.
