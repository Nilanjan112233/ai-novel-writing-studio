# NovelForge Phase D Writing Quality Study

## Evidence result

The eight labelled writing/continuity tasks split as follows:

- fact-only: 2/8 (25%);
- episode-required: 2/8 (25%);
- raw-evidence-required: 4/8 (50%).

Facts are sufficient for current ownership and POV knowledge boundaries. Episodes are required for emotional callbacks and credible relationship continuation. Raw prose is required for subtle foreshadowing, exact dialogue callbacks, tone comparison, and wording-specific emotional consistency.

## Does quality decrease when episodes are omitted?

At the evidence level, yes: 6/8 writing tasks lack required support with facts alone. A fact-only writer can remain logically correct yet lose causal and emotional continuity. For the callback cases, one compact episode is sufficient; loading several full chapters is unnecessary.

## Does raw evidence help?

Yes, for the difficult minority represented here. Exact prose distinguishes whether a clue was subtle or explicit, whether a callback repeats the earlier promise accurately, and whether two scenes share tone. Atomics and summaries cannot preserve diction or rhythm.

## Provider validation status

No new paid writing was generated. The audit is deliberately read-only, and existing Phase B/C provider outputs are not a matched fact/episode/raw prose ablation. Therefore this report does **not** claim non-inferior prose quality, hallucination rate, repetition, or emotional specificity.

## Required pre-production experiment

Run a small matched sample with the same writer, instruction, authority, word target, and provider for:

1. a long-range emotional callback;
2. a relationship-continuation scene;
3. a subtle-foreshadowing task;
4. an exact-dialogue contradiction task.

Blindly score continuity, callback naturalness, unnecessary recap, contradictions, knowledge leakage, repetition, and prose specificity. Until that passes, do not apply Phase D to writing or structured drafting.
