# RC5 Canon Workflow Walkthrough

## Discoverable path

1. Open **Canon** from the Govern group.
2. Browse, search, filter, inspect, edit, deactivate, or compare approved canon.
3. Select the visible **Review Canon Queue** action.
4. Inspect each AI suggestion’s confidence, evidence, affected locations, and downstream impact.
5. Approve or reject explicitly.
6. Approval updates the governed Canon registry and Story Bible.
7. Narrative Analysis refreshes, and resulting Story Health changes appear in Story Health History.

Canon and Canon Queue also have independent toolbox destinations. Page guidance explains the approval boundary on both surfaces. AI-generated facts never enter approved canon without the author’s decision.

RC5 replaces raw values and conflicts with author-facing value/evidence cards while preserving the existing Canon Governance service as the sole authority.
