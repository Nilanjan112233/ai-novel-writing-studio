# NovelForge Story Studio UX Report

## Result

Story Studio is now a persistent, author-facing conversation with a model selector, multiline composer, current grounded result treatment, historical labels, expandable evidence, planning suggestions, durable development status, and chapter/revision navigation.

## Browser acceptance

- The latest five completed interactions are immediately visible.
- Older history is collapsed behind `Show older` while preserving chronological order.
- Historical cards say `Historical conversation · saved response` and offer `Refresh against current story`.
- The selector is visible but explains that utility work stays task-routed.
- Story state at the top says the project has one chapter and identifies the latest approved chapter.
- The chapter library and writing/editorial destinations are reachable from the conversation.

## UX tradeoff

Result cards are still more structured than a pure chat product because NovelForge must show approvals, evidence, and recovery. They are now framed as conversational results rather than raw agent cards. Long AI work remains inline and durable, but specialist inspection pages remain available.
