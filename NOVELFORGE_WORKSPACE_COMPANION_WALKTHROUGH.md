# Workspace Companion Walkthrough

## Role

The Workspace Companion is a persistent, quiet guide inside the project workspace. It does not run a new story analyzer. It translates existing project events, Story Health, completeness gates, and proposal state into concise navigation and next-action prompts.

## Interaction

- The collapsed companion shows one relevant update without obscuring the workspace.
- Expanding it reveals the persistent message feed.
- A message can navigate directly to Story Health, Canon, Timeline, a chapter, or another relevant workspace section.
- Messages can be marked read or dismissed.
- The author can ask how a page works, why health is low, what to do next, or where a capability lives; the request is handed to the existing Project Assistant.

## Reactions

The companion responds to completed analysis, health movement, new unresolved mysteries, pending canon proposals, and newly reached production milestones. Stable source keys prevent repeated messages, and transient guidance is replaced rather than accumulated.

## Motion and attention

Animation is deliberately subtle and respects reduced-motion preferences. Milestones receive a short celebration, while routine information remains calm. The companion never moves over the Paragraph Editor or interrupts drafting with a modal.

## Example journey

1. Narrative Analysis completes and emits a project event.
2. Completeness reaches **World Ready**.
3. The companion adds a milestone message and suggests the next blocked criterion.
4. The author chooses the action and navigates to the relevant system.
5. After the issue is resolved, reanalysis updates Story Health and replaces stale guidance.
